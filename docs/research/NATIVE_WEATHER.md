# Native weather cycle

The playable world now runs `CWeather` instead of a static district weather row (`src/world/nativeWeather.ts`).

## Sources

- gta-reversed revision `0076f9017b4aeef7433957ac2bd8b6988e9df166`: `Weather.cpp` (`Init`, `FindWeatherRegion`, `FindWeatherTypesList`, `ForceWeather`, `ForceWeatherNow`, `ReleaseWeather`, `SetWeatherToAppropriateTypeNow`, `RenderRainStreaks`, `AddSandStormParticles`), `Data/Weather.def` (the five 64-entry hourly lists), `TimeCycle.cpp` (`CalcColoursForPoint` smog altitude blend and old/new interpolation), `Clouds.cpp` (low clouds, rainbow lines, streak gate, `MovingFogInit`, `MovingFog_Create`, `MovingFog_Update`, `MovingFogRender`), `PostEffects.cpp` (rain grain, `ImmediateModeRenderStatesSet`), `app/app_game.cpp` (`RenderEffects` order) and `Audio/Entities/AEWeatherAudioEntity.cpp`.
- `CWeather::Update` (0x72B850) is still `plugin::Call` upstream. The update follows Grinch_'s decompiled reimplementation (`Main.cpp` and its diff history, copies in the session research folder), with two corrections: the hour change reads `WeatherTypesList[WeatherTypeInList]` (the first revision's decompilation; a later edit replaced it with index 0), and rain/sandstorm step toward their targets by comparison (the decompiler left the flags uninitialised).

## Behaviour

- Every 16 frames and at each game-hour boundary the region is recomputed from the camera. At the boundary the previous target becomes the old weather and, unless forced or the camera is above 950 units, `WeatherTypeInList` advances and selects the region list entry. `InterpolationValue` is `(seconds / 60 + minutes) / 60`.
- Forced weather (the Lighting menu's weather selector in the playable world) calls `ForceWeatherNow`; "Automatic" releases it, so the list resumes at the next hour.
- Derived values: wet roads, rain and sandstorm (stepping 0.005 per native frame toward `((ms >> 13) & 3) * 0.1 + 0.7` times rainyness), cloud coverage, foggyness (foggy SF and sandstorm), SF foggyness, extra sunnyness, rainbow (cloudy → sunny in the first half hour, 07–20 h), sun glare, heat haze, traffic light brightness and headlight spectrum, plus lightning bursts and thunder timing when both endpoints are rainy.
- The timecycle samples old and new weather hour rows, blends the LA smog rows toward their clear rows by `(cameraZ - 20) / 200`, then interpolates by `InterpolationValue`. Sky foggyness, low cloud visibility `1 - max(foggyness, cloud coverage, extra sunnyness)` and the extra-sunny streak (both endpoints) read the native values.
- Rendering: `RenderRainStreaks` (32 integer-positioned streaks, strength `Rain * 110`, alpha fade by 2–4 per frame), the rain grain post effect (256² random grey raster at `SCREEN_STRETCH_X(1.5)`, strength stepping toward `Rain * 128`, drawn at `/4`), `Render_MaybeRenderRainbows` (six coronastar lines 100 units north), FX wind/rain for `FX_INFO_EMWEATHER` and `FX_INFO_WIND`, sandstorm particles and grass bending from `WindClipped`.
- Wet roads reach the cars: the player's car multiplies each wheel's adhesion by `GetWetMultiplier` (SURFINFO.DAT wet grip as a signed byte, `× 0.01 × WetRoads + 1`), and `DoBurstAndSoftGroundRatios` sinks wheels on SAND surfaces less as the roads get wet (docs/research/HANDLING.md).
- Moving SF fog (`src/render/NativeMovingFog.ts`): 350 sprite slots. `MovingFog_Update` fills every empty slot at the camera plus random (±58, ±58, ±5) with size 4–10, intensity 1, maximum intensity 8–20 and speed 0.5–1.2; occupied sprites drift by the fixed (0.06, 0.06) wind times their speed each frame, gain one time step of intensity within 60 units of the camera and lose one beyond it, freeing the slot at zero. `MovingFogRender` skips the pass without `Foggyness_SF` or indoors, steps `CurrentFogIntensity` by `TimeStep / 300` (toward 0 in no-rain zones, 1 elsewhere, 0 underwater), and draws camera-facing `cloudmasked` quads from particle.txd (`TheCamera` right, which RenderWare points screen-left, and up; UVs (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0,1)) tinted `min(sky bottom + 132, 255)` with alpha `trunc(Foggyness_SF × intensity × CurrentFogIntensity)`, alpha blended with depth test, no depth write, no fog, no culling, linear filtering and clamped addressing, after the FX pass and before the rain streaks. The update runs after the draw, once per native 30 fps frame (the wind drift is per frame, not per time step).

## Gaps

- `Windyness` (0x8D5E50) is not available locally. `WINDYNESS_BY_WEATHER` uses reVC's per-category values (sunny 0.25, cloudy 0.7, rainy 1, foggy/extra sunny 0, sandstorm uses the hurricane 2) as related-game evidence. The gust tables behind `WindDir` are also unavailable; the base north-east direction `WindClipped * 0.7` is used without gusts.
- `CWeather::AddRain` is not reversed: ground splashes around the camera use `prt_splash` with the FxPrtMult of the reversed ped/vehicle rain splashes; the rate is a reconstruction. Sandstorm particles are added with probability `Sandstorm` per frame.
- Rain, thunder and wind audio run through the native sound manager (docs/research/NATIVE_AUDIO.md) at 0 dB event volumes because EVENTVOL.DAT is not local.
- The fog's no-rain test uses the interior area in place of `CCullZones::CamNoRain`/`PlayerNoRain`, as the rain effects do.
- Not yet implemented: city-noise audio, heat haze, lightning sky flash, in-tunnel and underwater weather, cull-zone no-rain areas and headlight/traffic-light consumers.

## Verification

`tests/native-weather.test.ts` covers the LA list and hour boundary, the 950-unit freeze, forced rain ramp and derived values, cloudy→sunny rainbow, SF fog and sandstorm, and a scripted lightning burst with thunder timing. `tests/native-moving-fog.test.ts` covers slot creation around the camera, the pre-update draw and wind drift, fade-out and respawn beyond 60 units, `CurrentFogIntensity` in no-rain zones, underwater and indoors, 30 Hz pacing, corner order, UVs and the sky tint. In-game probes forced rainy countryside (grey timecycle, grain, streaks, splashes), a sandstorm (brown fog row with sand particles) and foggy SF weather (350 sprites, maximum alpha 19, tint from the sky bottom colour; a screenshot with the fog hidden differs by 5 grey levels on average, with the wisps depth-tested behind nearby geometry) without page errors.
