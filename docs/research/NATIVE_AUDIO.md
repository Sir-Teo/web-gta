# Native audio engine

The playable world runs a port of San Andreas' sound manager over Web Audio (`src/audio/native/`). Vehicles, aircraft, weather, weapons, explosions, car collisions, bullet hits and the player's footsteps go through it; the older sample mixer in `src/audio/AudioSystem.ts` only remains as a fallback until the banks load.

## Transcribed behaviour

- **CAESound**: volume minus headroom (bank headroom / 100), directional mike attenuation (0 dB in front, −6 dB behind), the 1,280-entry distance table by distance / roll-off, doppler `340 / (clamp(v, −35, 35) + 340)` from per-frame camera distances, camera-relative positions (listener pulled up to 0.5 units toward the player, 2 units behind first-person cameras), speed variance, play-time integration and loop wrap for virtual sounds, lifespan tied to entities, and `UpdateParameters`/`SoundHasFinished` callbacks.
- **CAESoundManager::Service**: 300 virtual sounds, 47 physical channels (48 software-mixed DirectSound channels minus the background stream), start-percentage conversion, uncancellable sounds kept first, the rest ordered by listener volume, stop/start of physical channels and per-frame volume, frequency and position updates.
- **CAEAudioHardware**: `RescaleChannelVolumes` (loudest channel normalised to 0 dB, group maxima released 1.2 dB per frame, gain sum limit `(6.4 − unduckable × 0.8) × 16383 / sum`), `PlaySound` start positions, static channel loops from the bank loop offset (samples), 28–30 ms smooth fades on seek starts and stops. The DirectSound listener has roll-off and doppler factors of 0, so Web Audio panners only pan (equal-power) by camera-space direction.
- **Bank slots**: `CAEMP3BankLoader` slots keep their bank until the new one is read; ten dummy engine slots are shared by reference count (`RequestBankSlot`, `DemandBankSlot`, `StoppedUsingBankSlot`); the player engine bank loads into slot 40 on entry.
- **CAEVehicleAudioEntity** for automobiles: dummy engines (idle/cruise crossfades with the native fade curves, engine start), the player engine state machine (`PLAYER_ID`, `PLAYER_WHEEL_SPIN`, `PLAYER_AC_FULL` with acceleration-loop gear steps, `PLAYER_CRZ`), idle/rev/cruise/acceleration volume and frequency curves, road noise by surface (SURFAUD.DAT grass/wet flags), reverse gear whine, skid twin loops by wheel state and surface, flat tyre, engine damage steam, rain drops on the body, horns (eight AI horn patterns, `HornType`/`HornPitch`), sirens and fast sirens, and door open/close sounds by door type.
- **Aircraft**: helicopters (player/dummy rotor front/rear/near/distant sounds, tilt and rotor-speed frequency curves, damaged-rotor variance; AI helis and the cop-heli bank), propeller planes (prop speed curve, stall bands) and jets (`ProcessGenericJet`).
- **CAEWeaponAudioEntity**: `PlayGunSounds` per weapon (dry-fire and low layers at the gun, the main shot at the gun plus a front-end stereo pair that carries the near field within 5 units and crossfades out by 12, rolled-off stereo tails pitched three semitones apart at random, 25 ms retrigger guard, aircraft gun variations), reloads, flamethrower/spray can/extinguisher loops with their 300 ms keep-alive, minigun spin/fire/stop, chainsaw idle/active/cutting/stop states in the player engine slot, camera shutter, goggles and knife stealth-kill sounds. **CAEGlobalWeaponAudioEntity**: rocket launches, aircraft guns (`AE_WEAPON_FIRE_PLANE`, minigun reported as M4) and the ambient services: distant pistol/AK bursts in Los Santos, the San Fierro fog horns in fog and the Las Venturas waterfall. **CAEExplosionAudioEntity**: three positional layers and a rolled-off front-end boom. **CAEPedAudioEntity**: player footsteps by SURFAUD.DAT class (generic bank for concrete, surface banks loaded into the player footstep slot, swimming on water) with `CPed::DoFootLanded` levels (walk −12 dB/0.9, run −6 dB/1.1, sprint 0 dB/1.2, crouched −18 dB/0.8), and landing sounds after falls.
- **CAECollisionAudioEntity**: `ReportCollision` from each car's contact manifolds (the other car as `SURFACE_CAR`, pedestrians as `SURFACE_PED`, world colliders by their COL surface; a car resting on a building face within 53° of its up axis becomes `SURFACE_RUBBER` unless SURFINFO.DAT gives that surface sparks), one-shot impacts from the 195-entry collision lookup that start later in the sample for softer impulses (`startAt = 100 − ParamD × impulse × 5`, clamped by the surface's maximum start with a matching volume drop), scrapes that loop while contact is reported (grass, water, metal or gravel loops by SURFAUD.DAT class, speed and volume from the car's move and turn speeds, stopped 100 ms after the last report), and `ReportBulletHit` samples by class (peds, water, wood, metal by incidence angle, gravel/concrete/tile, default). `ReportWaterSplash` for vehicles plays the collision bank's splash pair (67 and 66 at 0.94 speed, roll-off 2.5, `20·log10(min(0.6, |v|))` dB but no quieter than −18) when an aircraft's buoyancy spray is reported while it falls faster than 0.1 units per frame, unless its own splash is still playing. SURFAUD.DAT now loads with the playable world's surface files.
- **CAEFrontendAudioEntity** pickup cases (the function is still a plugin call upstream; its commented decompilation is followed): weapon pickups play the FRONTEND_GAME weapon pair (27/28), health, armour, adrenaline and money the money pair (16/17), bribes and info the info pair (14/15), and horseshoes, oysters and snapshots the part-mission pair (18/19), each as front-end stereo sounds behind a five-frame guard.
- **CAEWeatherAudioEntity**: left/right rain twin loops (swapping every 65–350 ms), cycling rain drops, rain volume stepping 0.5 dB per frame toward `20·log10((Rain − 0.2) / 0.8)`, thunder cracks and roll-off shaped by `LightningDuration`, and wind loops with gusts.
- The engine services at 30 Hz, the default frame limiter (`RsGlobal.frameLimit = APP_MAX_FPS`), so per-frame fades, steps and horn patterns keep their native pacing.

## Reconstructions and gaps

- **EVENTVOL.DAT is not local.** `CAEAudioEntity::GetDefaultVolume` reads 45,401 signed dB bytes from it; every event currently uses 0 dB. This is the largest remaining fidelity gap in the mix (rain, horns, sirens and doors are relative to it). `NativeAudioEngine.eventVolumes` accepts the table once it is available.
- The decompiled dummy engine transitions only update loops that already exist, which would leave a car silent after its first idle↔cruise crossfade; the port starts the incoming loop (rev at −100 dB, idle at −50 dB) as the fade curves imply.
- `CVehicle::ProcessSirenAndHorn` is not reversed: the player's horn follows reVC's branch over CPad's five-frame horn history (hold for the horn, tap to toggle the siren on siren vehicles). The horn is on Caps Lock or H while driving.
- Traffic horns: `CAutomobile::PlayHornIfNecessary` and `PlayCarHorn` are transcribed (the signed-char horn timer, 45-frame patterns, no horn at red lights). The flags that trigger them are set in `CCarCtrl`'s slow-down functions, which are not reversed; as in reVC's `m_bSlowedDownBecauseOfCars/Peds`, a traffic car raises them while it brakes for another vehicle or a pedestrian (including the player) in its lane, not for walls. The driver's `CTX_GLOBAL_BLOCKED` line is not played (no speech).
- `WHEELSPIN_*` rates use reVC's values (1, 10, 0.95, 0.7). Aircraft use the browser throttle as rotor/prop speed. The heli near sound uses the body position instead of `COMPONENT_WING_RR`.
- The wind update's decompiled gust assignments are garbled; the port steps the loop toward `21·ratio − 33` dB and its frequency toward the gust, never below 1.
- `PlayGunSounds`' decompiled crossfade divides by 12/r and then subtracts 5/r (a negative fraction); the port crossfades between 5 and 12 units. `ServiceAmbientGunFire` only fires bursts within 24 units of the player although it places them 40–80 units from the camera; the port plays bursts farther than 24 units. `HandleFootstepEvent` tests the generic bank in every slot, which would mute the player's surface banks; the requested bank is tested.
- `CAEPedAudioEntity::HandlePedHit` and the chainsaw event callers are not reversed: melee hits play the weapon bank strike samples, and a held chainsaw idles on foot and revs while attacking.
- `CPed::PlayFootSteps` is not reversed. Street pedestrians report footsteps at the foot-down windows of its commented decompilation (T/15 and T/2 + T/15 into the walk, run or sprint clip), with `DoFootLanded` levels by move state and the generic footstep bank that other peds always use. Peds that the browser creates in one batch start their first walk cycle at a random point, standing in for the native one-ped-per-frame population additions that keep them out of step. The player's footsteps still follow the movement phase.
- `CAECollisionAudioEntity::UpdateParameters` is not reversed; finished one-shots leave the sound list so the next impact starts a new one-shot. Rapier contacts report every step, so only contacts closing on each other before the step (as in `ApplyCollision`) are reported, with the normal impulse per unit mass as the native `fCollisionImpact`.
- Not ported yet: city noise (needs world line-of-sight whooshes), radio, boats, bikes, trains, RC vehicles, nitro and moving parts, glass and fire entities, ped and car water splashes, ped speech and pain.

## Verification

- `tests/native-audio.test.ts`: attenuation table, mike, camera space and doppler; 60 sounds on 47 channels with the loudest physical; one-shot completion callbacks; `RescaleChannelVolumes` normalisation and release; twin-loop swaps; dummy car start, crossfades and restart after stopping; sirens, fast sirens, AI horn patterns and the siren toggle; the player car reaching `PLAYER_AC_FULL` on its loaded engine bank; helicopter rotors; rain loops and wind.
- `tests/native-audio.test.ts` also covers pedestrian footsteps, gunshot layering and near/far front-end levels, explosions, player footsteps loading the grass bank, the chainsaw idle loop, impact start positions, the scrape loop and its 100 ms stop, bullet-hit sample classes and the pickup pairs with their frame guard.
- `tests/browser/vehicle-life.spec.ts`: traffic engines play through the native manager within 47 channels, stop servicing while paused and resume. `tests/browser/combat-audio.spec.ts`: M4 fire plays weapon-slot sounds, pausing stops the engine and muting suppresses new weapon sounds.
- In-game probes (not committed): twelve walking Grove Street pedestrians producing 2–8 overlapping generic footsteps while the player stood still; running and sprinting footsteps on concrete, an M4 burst with bullet-hit sounds, a Greenwood crash at 18 m/s with impact and scrape sounds; Greenwood idle → wheel spin → acceleration loop, horn; Maverick rotor frequency ramp; Rustler prop; Shamal jet sounds; forced rain loops; a Los Santos police car's siren when it switched to its close pursuit mission. No page errors.
