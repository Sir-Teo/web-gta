Native geometry, textures, animations and sound samples are used; gameplay controllers are authored approximations unless documented otherwise. The target is original PC 1.0 (user decision, September 17, 2026). Implemented, source-backed and reference-matched are separate states. The legacy implementation column below includes historical claims and is not proof of one-to-one compatibility. See [the current fidelity audit](FIDELITY_AUDIT.md).

## Blocked by reference stubs (gta-reversed ead3051, checked 2026-09-20)

These behaviours were surveyed for porting and found to rest on functions the reference still calls into the
executable (`plugin::Call` / `reversed = false`), so a faithful port is not possible from it:

- Siren coronas and vehicle light sprites: `CAutomobile::PreRender`, `CVehicle::DoVehicleLights` (the browser's
  `SirenLights` stays an authored approximation).
- Point lights on peds, cars and objects: `CPointLights::AddLight/GenerateLightsAffectingObject/RenderFogEffect`.
- Wheel dirt, grass, gravel, sand and spray particles: `Fx_c::AddWheelGrass/Gravel/Mud/Dust/Sand/Spray`.
- Cars-fly cheat and any car flight: `CVehicle::FlyingControl` (only the sketch is present).
- Towing and trailers: `CVehicle::UpdateTrailerLink/UpdateTractorLink` (the hitch geometry and `TowTruckControl`
  are reversed, the link physics is not).
- Trains and trams: most of `CTrain` (and `tracks*.dat` is not in the content set).
- Camera modes: `CCam::Process_FollowPedWithMouse/Process_FollowCar_SA/Process_AimWeapon` (`reversed = false`).
- Breath bar placement and visibility: `CHud::DrawVitalStats` (`RenderBreathBar` itself is reversed).
- Fat / muscle walk styles: `CPlayerPed::ProcessAnimGroups`.
- Fire truck and ambulance dispatch: `CCarCtrl::GenerateEmergencyServicesCar`, `GenerateOneEmergencyServicesCar`
  and the go-to-coordinates steering (`CTaskComplexDriveFireTruck` and `CTaskComplexUseWaterCannon` are reversed).
- Ped-to-ped avoidance detours: `CPedGeometryAnalyser::ComputeRouteRoundSphere` (the
  `CTaskComplexAvoidOtherPedWhileWandering` shell is reversed).
- Armed peds taking cover: `CTaskComplexKillPedOnFootArmed::CreateNextSubTask` and `CFormation::
  FindCoverPointsBehindBox` (the `CCover` registry is reversed but its selection code looks miscompiled).
- Vehicle blob shadows: `CShadows::StoreShadowForVehicle` is a raw call whose body exists only as an unfinished
  comment sketch in the reference, so it is not ported (cars keep contact lighting only).
- Vehicle component culling and LOD: `CVisibilityPlugins::ShouldCullVehicleAtomic` and the hi/lo detail render
  callbacks are reversed, and their distances follow from the camera multipliers (the component cull distance is
  the square of twenty times the LOD multiplier, the hi-detail distance twice the square of seventy times the
  generation multiplier), but the choice of dot product depends on each atomic's component flags, which
  `CVehicleModelInfo::SetVehicleComponentFlags` takes from `ms_vehicleDescs`, a table the reference only points at
  by address (0x8A7740). Without that table the per-atomic flags cannot be derived, so the browser keeps drawing
  every vehicle atomic.
- Water creatures (fish, jellyfish, turtles, dolphins): `WaterCreature_c` and `WaterCreatureManager_c` are reversed,
  but the seven-entry tuning table `ms_waterCreatureInfos` (models, group sizes, spawn depths, speeds) is a
  binary address (0x8D3698) the reference only points at, so the creatures are not ported.
- Rail-track drag: `CVehicle::ms_fRailTrackResistance` default (exe data).
- Ambulance and fire truck crews in traffic: the random car groups never carry them and
  `CCarCtrl::GenerateEmergencyServicesCar` is a stub; the default medic and fireman model tables are exe data.
- Crosshair growth while firing: `m_fAttackButtonCounter` never increments in reversed code.
- Follow-up (not a stub): civilian passengers are not on the seated-sphere bullet path yet (traffic drivers,
  police crews and drive-by crews are).

## Where the next round should start (2026-09-20)

The survey above found every self-contained behaviour either stubbed in the reference or waiting on a trigger the
browser does not raise. The productive vein instead is the script runtime: a census of `main.scm` against the
runtime's dispatch shows 1,190 distinct commands it does not handle, and many of them reach engine routines that
are fully reversed even where the command's own dispatch is not. The restart points, stunt jumps and parked car
generators in this round were exactly that shape, and each one is verifiable against the script itself.

Six families have gone in on that basis: the restart points, the stunt jumps, the parked car generators, the
script's objects, the zone population settings and the camera. Running INITIAL from its restart points now
carries through its objects and stops at ROTATE_OBJECT, a timed rotation the reference does not reverse; the
block's other gaps are the property pickups (blocked: `CPickups::GenerateNewOne` is a raw call),
SET_CLOSEST_ENTRY_EXIT_FLAG, DEACTIVATE_GARAGE (most of CGarages is stubbed) and CREATE_PICKUP.

Coverage over the published `main.scm`, counting every decoded instruction of MAIN and all 135 mission blocks:
the runtime now handles 274,570 of 360,439 instructions, 76.2%, across 342 distinct opcodes, with 1,113 distinct
opcodes still unhandled, and `tests/scm-coverage.test.ts` holds those figures so they cannot quietly drop. Ten
families went in this round: the restart points, stunt jumps, parked car generators, script objects, zone
population, camera, sprites and boxes, the small engine statics, the script fires and the wanted level.

Of the commands the runtime still does not handle, 325 have a reversed handler. The largest group by far
is the char commands, led by IS_CHAR_DEAD at 7,700 uses, and all of them work through a CPed the runtime cannot
create: CREATE_CHAR, CREATE_CHAR_INSIDE_CAR and the other constructors are among the handlers the reference
leaves to the executable. The mission audio slots looked reachable at about 3,500 uses, but every one of
`CAEScriptAudioEntity`'s mission audio methods is marked unreversed in the reference, so that family is blocked
at the level that matters, on top of the missing speech banks. What is left reachable without an entity pool is
the script fires, whose `CFireManager` is reversed in full, and the remaining one-field commands.
